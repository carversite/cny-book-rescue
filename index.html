Questions

Unwanted book pickup, made straightforward.

Does CNY Book Rescue pick up unwanted books?

Yes. CNY Book Rescue offers free at-home pickup for unwanted books throughout Central New York.

What types of books and media do you accept?

We consider fiction, nonfiction, hardcover books, paperbacks, children's books, textbooks, cookbooks, history books, biographies, reference books, DVDs, CDs, cassette tapes, Blu-rays, video games, and large collections. We can also take unwanted clothing. Acceptance depends on type and condition.

Is this helpful for moving, downsizing, or estate cleanouts?

Yes. The service is a good fit for cleaning out bookshelves, moving, downsizing, estate cleanouts, storage cleanouts, and large book collections. In many cases, you can leave approved boxes on your porch or another accessible pickup spot.
