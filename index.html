Give Your Books a Second Chapter

CNY Book Rescue helps Central New York residents clear out unwanted books with free, convenient pickup from your home, porch, garage, or doorstep.

Request a Book Pickup

Simple, helpful, local

Have books taking up space? Box them up, set them out, and we'll pick them up.

Whether you're moving, downsizing, cleaning out an estate, or simply making room on your shelves, CNY Book Rescue makes it easy to give unwanted books a second life without hauling heavy boxes yourself.

After we coordinate the pickup, you can usually place approved boxes on your porch or another accessible spot and let us handle the rest.

Service Area

Free Pickup Throughout Central New York

CNY Book Rescue provides free pickup of unwanted books throughout Syracuse, Utica, Rome, Oneida, Herkimer and many surrounding Central New York communities.

We also serve many nearby communities across the Syracuse-Utica corridor. If you're unsure whether you're in our pickup area, submit a pickup request with your city or ZIP code and we'll let you know if service is available.

Questions

Unwanted book pickup, made straightforward.

Does CNY Book Rescue pick up unwanted books?

Yes. CNY Book Rescue offers free at-home pickup for unwanted books throughout Central New York.

What types of books and media do you accept?

We consider fiction, nonfiction, hardcover books, paperbacks, children's books, textbooks, cookbooks, history books, biographies, reference books, DVDs, CDs, cassette tapes, Blu-rays, video games, and large collections. We can also take unwanted clothing. Acceptance depends on type and condition.

Is this helpful for moving, downsizing, or estate cleanouts?

Yes. The service is a good fit for cleaning out bookshelves, moving, downsizing, estate cleanouts, storage cleanouts, and large book collections. In many cases, you can leave approved boxes on your porch or another accessible pickup spot.
