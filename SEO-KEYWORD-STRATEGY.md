# CNY Book Rescue SEO Keyword Strategy

This document is developer-facing SEO planning material for `cnybookrescue.com`. It is not public page copy and should not be rendered as hidden keyword text.

## Positioning

CNY Book Rescue is a static public service website for free pickup of unwanted books, accepted media, and currently accepted clothing in Central New York. The site should reach people who need to clear books and accepted items without falsely claiming CNY Book Rescue is a junk-removal company, estate-sale company, estate liquidator, charity, nonprofit, church, library, school, realtor, property manager, college, or tax-deductible donation organization.

Primary themes:

- Free book pickup
- Unwanted books
- Used books and old books
- Book donation pickup in ordinary search-language usage
- Book removal and book haul-away for accepted items
- Large book collections
- Inherited books and personal libraries
- Moving, downsizing, estate cleanouts, storage cleanouts, and home cleanouts
- Accepted physical media including DVDs, CDs, Blu-rays, cassette tapes, movies, music, and video games
- Clothing, clothes, coats, jackets, winter clothing, and winter wear while clothing remains an accepted category

## Business-Accuracy Guardrails

Do not publish or imply any of the following unless the business status changes and is legally verified:

- CNY Book Rescue is a junk-removal company.
- CNY Book Rescue is an estate-sale company.
- CNY Book Rescue is an estate liquidator.
- CNY Book Rescue is affiliated with Catholic Charities.
- CNY Book Rescue is affiliated with a church, parish, school, library, college, university, realtor, or property-management company.
- CNY Book Rescue is a nonprofit, charity, or 501(c)(3).
- Items are tax-deductible donations.
- CNY Book Rescue has a public office or physical drop-off location in any listed community.
